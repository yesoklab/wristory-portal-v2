# WRISTORY ($WR) Official Whitepaper v2.0
**The Decentralized Digital Heritage & Time Archive on Ethereum Layer-2 (Base) and Tezos**

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Vision
WRISTORY is a decentralized cultural heritage preservation and digital archive platform designed to immutably record historical narratives, patriotic legacies, and generative digital art on-chain. Combining smart dial ecosystems (Wear OS) and blockchain, WRISTORY ensures eternal ownership and appreciation of historical moments.

Following 1+ years of building upon Tezos with 1,063 loyal holders, WRISTORY is officially expanding to **Coinbase's Ethereum Layer-2 (Base Mainnet)** to overcome liquidity limitations and unlock global DeFi depth via **Uniswap v4**.
