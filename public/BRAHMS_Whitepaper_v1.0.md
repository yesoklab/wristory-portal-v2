불암스BRAHMS ($BRAHMS) Official Whitepaper v1.0
The On-Chain Cultural Heritage Memorial, Digital Archive & Child Welfare Initiative on Base Layer-2
1. Executive Summary & Vision
불암스BRAHMS ($BRAHMS) is a decentralized digital cultural heritage tribute and community initiative deployed on Coinbase's Ethereum Layer-2 (Base Mainnet).
The project is dedicated to permanently archiving the cultural contributions, humanist philosophy, and public service legacy of Choi Bool-am (최불암), one of the most beloved and iconic cultural figures in modern Korean history.
For over five decades, Mr. Choi's warm, comforting presence has touched generations through landmark cultural works such as Country Diaries (전원일기) and Chief Inspector (수사반장). Furthermore, as the national patron and long-standing chairperson of ChildFund Korea (초록우산 어린이재단) for over 40 years, he has dedicated his life to child welfare, vulnerable youth, and societal compassion.
불암스BRAHMS translates this enduring humanitarian legacy into an immutable on-chain record on Base, creating a sustainable foundation for cultural preservation, youth artist empowerment, and social welfare support.
